Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Vidya AI.

What is Vidya AI by ExtraaEdge?+
Vidya AI is the Intelligent Admissions Growth Platform from ExtraaEdge, built for education. It combines four AI products — VidyaGPT (24×7 web counselling), VidyaWABA GPT (WhatsApp), VidyaPulse (AI lead scoring), and VidyaAI Voice Agent (automated calling and call coaching) — on one platform used by 500+ education brands across India.
How fast does VidyaGPT respond to student enquiries?+
VidyaGPT responds to every enquiry in under 3 seconds, 24×7, on both your website and WhatsApp — compared with an industry-average institutional response time of 14–18 hours. Speed is the difference between converting a high-intent lead and losing them to a competitor overnight.
Is VidyaGPT just another chatbot?+
No. VidyaGPT runs on an education-specific Small Language Model trained on admissions — programs, fees, eligibility and campus life. Unlike generic chatbots that match keywords and answer FAQs, it counsels students with full context and personalisation, and is built to avoid hallucinations.
How does VidyaPulse decide which leads are HOT?+
VidyaPulse reads 50+ signals per student — website visits, time on site, email opens and clicks, WhatsApp activity, inbound calls, campus visits and application stage — and produces a HOT / WARM / COLD score refreshed every 24 hours, plus a specific next-best action for each lead.
Will students mind talking to an AI voice agent?+
In tested calls, over 70% of students couldn't tell the difference. Students care about getting an answer, not who's calling. Vidya Call handles repetitive RSVP and follow-up calls so your counsellors are free for the conversations that genuinely need a human.
How long does implementation take?+
Most institutions go live in under 10 days — the fastest in admissions technology — with 95% counsellor adoption within four weeks. Vidya AI is built for your current admission cycle, not the next one.
What ROI can we expect?+
Customers report 50% better conversion with VidyaGPT and up to 24× ROI (around ₹7.2 Cr net annual gain for an institution with 5,000 enquiries a year). Annapurna College improved lead-to-conversion from 2–3% to 10%; Uttaranchal University saw 38% application growth. Use the calculator above for a projection on your own numbers.
How is Vidya AI different from LeadSquared or Meritto?+
LeadSquared is a generic sales CRM that takes 3–6 months to implement; Meritto asks you to change your process to fit the software. Vidya AI is purpose-built for admissions, goes live in 7–10 days, adapts to how your team already works, and frees roughly 40% of counsellor time from day one.
Does VidyaWABA GPT use the official WhatsApp API?+
Yes. VidyaWABA GPT is built on the official WhatsApp Business API — fully compliant and opt-in managed — not an unofficial workaround. It scales from 10 to 100,000 conversations with the same intelligence as VidyaGPT on web.
