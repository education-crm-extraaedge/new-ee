What is SMS Marketing?

SMS marketing involves sending targeted text messages to prospects, students, or alumni to drive specific actions — from scheduling campus visits to fee reminders and course updates. Unlike email, SMS reaches users instantly and on their most personal device: their phone.

In the education sector, this translates to timely touchpoints across the entire student journey: awareness, inquiry, application, enrollment, and retention.

Benefits of SMS Marketing for Institutions

  • Instant delivery: Messages are typically delivered and read within 3 minutes.
  • High engagement: Response rates are ~45% vs. ~6% for email.
  • Cost-effective: Reach thousands of leads at a fraction of digital ad spend.
  • Personalised touchpoints: Send tailored messages based on program interest or inquiry stage.
  • Multi-channel synergy: Works best when combined with WhatsApp, email, and calls inside a unified CRM.

Key Strategies for SMS Marketing in Education

1. Segment your audience

Don't blast everyone with the same message. Segment by program interest, location, admission stage, and lead quality. ExtraaEdge's CRM lets you create dynamic segments that auto-update based on prospect behavior.

2. Time your messages wisely

Respect time zones and local cultural norms. Analysis of 1M+ messages shows that SMS sent between 10 AM–12 PM and 3 PM–6 PM have the highest open and response rates for education enquiries.

Personalisation Tips That Actually Work

Generic messages get ignored. Use dynamic tokens to personalise by name, course, campus, and stage. Here's what high-converting SMS looks like:

  • Use the prospect's first name — always.
  • Reference the specific program they inquired about.
  • Include a single, clear CTA: "Reply YES", "Click here", or "Call now".
  • Keep it under 160 characters to avoid splitting and additional billing.

Automation in SMS Campaigns

Manual SMS campaigns are unsustainable at scale. With ExtraaEdge's marketing automation, you can set up:

  1. Trigger-based SMS — fired automatically when a lead fills a form, attends a webinar, or downloads a brochure.
  2. Drip sequences — a pre-set series of messages spaced across the nurturing cycle.
  3. Re-engagement campaigns — auto-detect cold leads and re-ignite interest with value-driven nudges.

Compliance & Best Practices

SMS compliance is non-negotiable. Key considerations for India-based institutions:

  • Register sender IDs with TRAI-compliant telecom operators (DLT registration).
  • Always include an opt-out option ("Reply STOP to unsubscribe").
  • Maintain consent logs within your CRM for audit purposes.
  • Follow DND (Do Not Disturb) registry rules strictly.

Measuring SMS Campaign Results

Track these KPIs religiously to optimise your SMS efforts:

  • Delivery rate — percentage of messages successfully delivered.
  • Open / read rate — estimated via link click-throughs.
  • Response rate — replies or CTA completions.
  • Conversion rate — leads that progressed to the next funnel stage.
  • Opt-out rate — monitor to ensure messaging remains relevant.
