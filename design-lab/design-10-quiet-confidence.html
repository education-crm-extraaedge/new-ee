IAdmissions is not a sales pipeline

Most CRMs were built to sell software to salespeople and later repainted for education. The seams show: “deals” renamed “students”, quarterly quotas renamed “intakes”, and a data model that has never met an agent network, a parent phone call or a walk-in day.

We built for admission offices only. Enquiry seasons that triple volume in six weeks. Counsellors who carry two hundred conversations each. Decisions made by families, not procurement teams. When the tool matches the work, the work stops fighting the tool; that is the whole trick, and it is why 95% of counsellors are still using the platform after go-live.

IISpeed is a kindness before it is a metric

A student who enquires at 9:41 pm is not a lead in a queue. They are a person at their most hopeful, comparing three futures in one browser. Making them wait until Monday is not a process problem first; it is a discourtesy, and discourtesy converts poorly.

So the AI layer, Vidya AI, answers in about 60 seconds, at any hour, in 95+ languages: real answers from your own brochure and fee rules, then a booked counsellor callback. VidyaPulse reads the conversation and scores intent; VidyaAgents calls to confirm. Institutions that switch measure up to 90% faster first response, and conversion lifts of up to 40%. The numbers follow the courtesy. They usually do.
