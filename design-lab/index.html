ExtraaEdge Design Lab · internal review

10 homepages, 10 strategies

Each prototype is a complete, self-contained homepage built on the same raw material: the real brand system (Inter, #19335D, #DE6E30), the canonical numbers (500+ institutions, 10M+ enquiries, 60-second response, up to 40% lift, 4.7/5 from 320+ teams, 7-day go-live, 95% adoption, 95+ languages, 50+ integrations), real customer names and videos, real logos. What differs is the strategy: information architecture, psychology, AI storytelling, trust placement, CTA logic and interaction model. No fabricated data anywhere; where proof does not exist, the design says so honestly.

How to review: open each on desktop and phone. Ask of each: which visitor intent does it serve best, and what does it sacrifice? These are strategy candidates for the next homepage, or sections of several can be combined onto one spine.
DESIGN 01

The First 60 Seconds

Signature: live race timeline in the hero

Problem-dramatization. The hero replays the visitor's nightmare (an enquiry arriving, unanswered) as a two-lane timeline: typical office vs Vidya AI. Loss aversion does the selling; the page then explains, proves and closes.

Best for
Problem-aware directors
Psychology
Loss aversion, urgency without fake scarcity
Trust
Contextual: each claim's receipt beside it

Ask the Platform

Signature: the homepage behaves like VidyaGPT

Conversational, intent-routed. Six question chips = six buying stages; each answers in a chat bubble then routes to a full answer section. The site demonstrates the product's manner instead of describing it.

Best for
All six intent levels, self-served
Psychology
Agency: the visitor drives
Trust

The Funnel, Rebuilt

Signature: scrolling the page = walking the funnel

The IA is the admission funnel itself: six stages, each with its leak, its fix, a product visual and stage-local proof. A sticky rail tracks progress. The CTA is framed as the seventh stage.

Best for
Heads of admissions, process thinkers
Psychology
Recognition: "that is my funnel"
Trust
Per-stage micro-proof

Before / After

Signature: one master switch flips the whole page

Contrast strategy. Four familiar moments (9:41pm enquiry, counsellor's 10am, review meeting, peak week) each exist in two versions; a sticky toggle flips every scene between manual and AI. The delta is the pitch.

Best for
Solution-aware skeptics of "AI hype"
Psychology
Contrast effect, endowed progress
Trust
The tally band + one named voice

The Counsellor's Day

Signature: 8:59am to 2:11am timeline narrative

Human-centric. Follows one counsellor through a working day; AI appears only where it actually acts (orange pins). Answers the adoption objection structurally: the story is her keeping her job and losing her worst work.

Best for
Teams burned by abandoned CRMs
Psychology
Narrative transport, identification
Trust
95% adoption + counsellor-adjacent voice

The Evidence Ledger

Signature: claim left, receipt right, audit numbering

Skeptic-first. Seven claims, each paired with its evidence or its honest limit ("your baseline decides"). No adjectives. The CTA is reframed as the audit's next step: a verification demo.

Best for
Comparison shoppers, CFO-brains
Psychology
Credibility via restraint
Trust

The Operating Console

Signature: the homepage is the product's dark UI

Product-first immersion. Five annotated screens inside a console shell: dashboard, VidyaPulse queue, WhatsApp inbox, VidyaAgents log, automation rules. Marketing chrome reduced to one light band at the end.

Best for
Product-aware, rep-free evaluators (61% of B2B buyers)
Psychology
Show, don't tell, taken literally
Trust
Transparency of the product itself

Decision Paths

Signature: four seats, four tailored mini-pages

Committee-buying psychology. Director, admissions head, counsellor and IT each get their own panel: their outcomes, their objections, their proof, their CTA wording. The close reunites the committee in one demo.

Best for
Enterprise/university buying groups
Psychology
Relevance via self-selection
Trust
Role-matched proof per panel
Open design 08 → DESIGN 09

The Number

Signature: sticky projection bar fed by 3 sliders

ROI-anchored. A no-email calculator turns the visitor's own funnel into a projected admissions band; every section is framed as a "mover" of that number; the projection follows in a sticky bar to the CTA. Honesty block explains why it is a range.

Best for
Decision-ready, budget-owning buyers
Psychology
Personal relevance, commitment
Trust
The fine print as a feature

Quiet Confidence

Signature: the homepage as a 4-minute essay

Editorial anti-SaaS. Three numbered beliefs argued in prose, sparse interludes (one number, one logo line, one voice), the Architect Mindset as the spine, and exactly one door at the end. Zero cards, zero feature grids.

Best for
Founder-taste buyers, brand differentiation
Psychology
Scarcity of ask = authority
Trust
Restraint itself, plus one full voice
