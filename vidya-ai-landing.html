Frequently asked

Everything about VidyaAI.

What is VidyaAI? +
VidyaAI is an intelligent, AI-first admissions platform built for education. It answers, qualifies and follows up with every student across WhatsApp, web and phone in 95+ languages — and hands your counsellors only hot, ready-to-enrol leads. It combines VidyaGPT (chat), Vidya Pulse (intelligence) and Vidya Agent (automation).
Which languages does it support? +
95+ languages across chat and AI voice calls, including Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali, Gujarati, Malayalam, Punjabi and Urdu — so no enquiry is lost to a language barrier.
Does it work on WhatsApp? +
Yes. VidyaAI is an official WhatsApp Business API partner. It chats, sends brochures and forms, runs reminders and books counsellor calls — all inside WhatsApp.
How is it trained on our data? +
VidyaAI uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) trained exclusively on your secure, partner-specific data — websites, PDFs, brochures, fee structures and policies. This creates a private, hallucination-resistant assistant with hyper-accurate, institution-specific answers and source citations.
Does it replace our counsellors? +
No — it amplifies them. VidyaAI handles the repetitive top-of-funnel work 24/7 and hands counsellors only warm, fully-briefed, high-intent leads, so they spend their time closing and enrolling rather than chasing.
Is our data secure? +
Yes. VidyaAI is ISO 27001 certified and GDPR / FERPA / CCPA compliant, with end-to-end encryption, India data residency, role-based access, audit trails and customer-specific data isolation.
