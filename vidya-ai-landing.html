Curiosity Corner

Everything about VidyaAI.

What is VidyaAI? +
An intelligent, AI-first admissions platform built for education. It answers, qualifies and follows up with every student across WhatsApp, web and phone in 30+ languages — combining VidyaGPT (chat), Vidya Pulse (intelligence) and Vidya Agent (automation) — and hands counsellors only hot, ready-to-enrol leads.
Which languages does it support? +
30+ languages across chat and AI voice — all Indian regional languages (Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali, Gujarati, Malayalam, Punjabi, Urdu & more) plus Spanish, Arabic and German.
What training data does VidyaGPT use? +
Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) trained exclusively on your secure, partner-specific data — websites, PDFs, brochures, fee structures and policies — creating a private, hallucination-resistant assistant with source citations.
How does it prevent hallucinations? +
A rigorous fact-checking system verifies responses against your official documentation before delivery, and the Education SLM is designed to avoid common AI hallucinations in educational contexts — providing only verified information with citations.
Is it integrated with ExtraaEdge CRM? +
Yes — natively. Automated data sync, lead tracking and communication history give a unified view of every student interaction, with intent scores written back live.
Is our data secure? +
SOC 2 & ISO 27001, GDPR / FERPA / CCPA compliant, end-to-end encryption, India data residency, MFA, role-based access and regular penetration testing.
