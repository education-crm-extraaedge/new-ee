An Education CRM is a purpose-built system for managing student engagement across the entire enrollment lifecycle.

It is designed for high-volume, B2C admissions environments — enabling institutions to capture, track, and engage prospective students across every touchpoint through a unified, data-driven system.

Unlike traditional CRMs and B2B sales CRMs, ExtraaEdge's Education CRM is designed for scale and speed, supporting large lead volumes, multi-channel communication, and rapid decision cycles typical of student recruitment.

It equips admissions, counseling, and marketing teams with contextual engagement tools, automation, and real-time insights to prioritize high-intent students, improve follow-ups, and increase conversion rates.

VidyaGPT AI is embedded within ExtraaEdge Education CRM and acts as a real-time intelligence layer for counseling teams.

For every lead, it analyzes the complete lead profile — source, behavior, journey stage, communication history, and engagement signals — to generate contextual insights and next-best-action recommendations.

This enables teams to move from intuition-led decision-making to data-driven execution, ensuring every lead is prioritized, engaged, and progressed with precision.

By continuously learning from outcomes, VidyaGPT improves follow-ups, reduces response gaps, and standardizes high performance across teams — resulting in faster conversions, higher efficiency, and more predictable enrollment outcomes.

ExtraaEdge is natively built on AI. VidyaGPT acts as the core intelligence layer powering both student engagement and team performance through purpose-built agents embedded across the platform.

For your students: VidyaGPT Guide is an always-on, multilingual chat agent that delivers instant, context-aware responses. VidyaGPT Voice enables human-like automated calling to qualify and nurture leads.

For your teams: VidyaGPT Coach prioritizes leads and suggests next-best actions. VidyaGPT Assist helps navigate data and reports conversationally. VidyaGPT Filters allow users to query and segment data using natural language.

Together, VidyaGPT makes every student interaction smarter and every team hour more productive.
